# Authored expansion and constrained free hunts

These are independently authored implementations of the nine §11.3 expansion sketches, not additional Appendix A fixtures. The original GDD and the M01/P1 rules/readers remain unchanged. Development is authorised by the user's full-completion instruction; human/device/creative/listening acceptance remains deferred.

## Version and ownership

`src/expansion/content/*.json` defines each package at content **1.0.0**, engine **expansion-1**, rubric **expansion-1.0.0** and its own `mXX-scoped-response-1` policy. Private packages include finite worlds, facts, source matrices, sufficient sets and unsupported-assertion predicates. React imports only the separately authored public capability projection (`public.json`), a `View`, and a closed debrief. It cannot consult expected choices or hidden facts. Meaning-changing edits after publication require version/compatibility handling; do not reinterpret an old command log.

## Source and cutoff matrix

| Acquisition | Source and proposition | Static / dynamic | Scope and limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Starting record | Review briefing | Delivered at step 0 | States the initial question without classifying the actor |
| Baseline | Task/dependency catalogue | Static, available at 0 | Reveals relevant scopes, business consequence and task boundary; does not establish the current operator |
| Work context | Work-authorisation register | Static, available at 0 | Tests this session, target, task and period; a gap supports neither approval nor denial |
| Activity | Session audit | Dynamic, event 2 / available 2 in standard profile | Records the specific operation, not a person or motive |
| Independent transaction | Service-side journal | Dynamic, event 2 / available 2 in standard profile | Independently collects the operation; another card alone does not establish sufficiency |
| Verified business check | Pre-established directory callback | Request costs 1; due request-start +3 | Confirms work context or delivers a timeout; never identifies the session operator |
| Future window (M08/M11) | Controlled, harmless verification event | Activation costs 2; next advanced step generates event | Tests only the named target/capability/time, with no historical backfill |

Queries capture at request start, cost one step and deliver at completion. Availability during the charged step is excluded. Static reopens are free; dynamic later cutoffs cost one even when empty. Scope changes, evidence roles, comparison and reading cost zero. Pending duplicate checks do not reset their due time. Source collection and player acquisitions are separate ledgers.

## Causal resolution and authority

The world is fixed at creation. At step 2 a legitimate business operator (phase 2) or an unauthorised session operator (phase 3) attempts the specified operation with an active-access precondition. Healthy sources record in phase 5; queries/checks deliver in phase 6. The bounded further objective occurs at step 8 in the standard profile if the session still has access. Response completions are phase 1 and win an objective-tick tie. No UI reveals this private schedule before reconstruction.

The player may recommend restriction of the **named organisation-controlled session only**. The authorised owner validates a sufficient cited misuse set; the player cannot self-approve, isolate a host, disable an account or shut down a service. A continuing response request costs one and completes at request-start +2 in the standard profile. Duplicate pending/effective requests do not create another effect. A two-step handoff expectation begins only when an out-of-scope observation is delivered. The timing deduction remains confined to Decision's 30-point timing component.

## Evidence-based assessment

Weights remain **30/25/30/15**, with one final half-up rounding. Four fixed evidence questions cover lead/baseline, business context, technical context and a disposition-specific sufficient set. Reasoning tests a compatible competing explanation, observation-linked limits and the case's public investigation boundary. M02/M12 require a saved consequence-based priority order; M11 requires compatible roles for separate claims, including scope/limit roles for collection gaps. Reporting names scope, confidence, owner and follow-up; M12 retains a separate coverage-restoration task.

Per full observable family, two strong routes are authored:

1. Opening + baseline + specific work record + scoped session audit.
2. Opening + baseline + verified business response + independent service transaction.

The two routes are not generic “two-source” credit: all four propositions must be supported. Full benign/misuse variants have different factual work/action observations. Sparse benign/malicious twins have literally identical public records, availability, timing, guidance and quality. Visibility routes use explicit archive/activity gaps or a verified timeout plus independent capability gap. A bounded preliminary route can hand off the outstanding evidence question before every source is acquired. Unsupported guesses remain low credit.

Confidence is about the activity claim. A tightly explained scoped action can use medium/high, a supported outside-approval finding uses medium, and visibility/preliminary findings use low. These scenarios do not establish a human operator or malicious motive. Explicit unsupported person, intent, authority, causal and global-coverage assertions cannot become supported by a lucky hidden actor; malformed references remain rejected rather than graded. Optional prose is ungraded.

Assessment and known evidence freeze at closure. Continuation can deliver a pending check or resolve a control but cannot add evidence to the original score. Debrief filters by delivery, shows recorded relationships, distinguishes knowable/missing/after-handoff material and reveals the world/collection/acquisition tracks only on request. Same-conditions replay is labelled as practice after a reveal.

## Reproduction and recovery

The separate **threat-hunter-expansion**, schema **1**, portable manifest pins engine/content/rubric/policy/profile, fixture hash, private world, mode, seed and generator. `threat-hunter-expansion-v1` stores separate active/previous/original/completed campaign rows and a separate `free` slot. Existing M01 and P1 rows are never migrated into this namespace. Full snapshot/prefix audit, owned replay, 2 MiB bounds, deterministic exports, receipt acknowledgement after transaction completion, complete-row CAS, archives and raw-original recovery apply.

The preloaded worker performs browser preparation/audit, with a bounded unavailable state and retry; it never awaits inside a Dexie transaction. Web Locks are slot-scoped. Browsers without coordination offer read/export or explicit temporary play. Closed volatile runs require export acknowledgement before replacement. Every new variation archives the exact durable closed run first, and switching screens keeps loaded controllers mounted.

## Seeded free hunts

Generator **causal-templates-xorshift32-1** selects a validated mission template, its world/collection family, and one independently selected declared profile. Records derive from that causal selection; there is no independently random evidence list or runtime LLM. Standard, late-collection (+2 source availability and objective steps) and slow-response (+1 response step) profiles change meaningful opportunity/coverage conditions. Public policy discloses the active source/response delays without exposing the actor.

The complete manifest and command history reproduce the run. Import recomputes the seed selection and rejects mismatched template/world/profile metadata. The fixed 100-seed corpus exercises all nine templates, four worlds and three profiles. Free hunts keep their own active slot and archives and do not falsely complete a named campaign case. Achievements/profile examples are descriptive local observations, never a professional diagnosis or leaderboard.

## Candidate presentation and acceptance

Each case uses the existing versioned Northstar environmental crops and shared finished surface/focus tokens, with its own workspace treatment. Each active optional pack contains **21 entries**: own voiced opening/first guidance, 14 literal shared neutral speech files, and five unchanged M01 sound/ambience files. First guidance uses immutable v2 clips (4–12 second target); original v1 candidates and both lossless master sets remain retained. Speech, captions and scripts are identical; full-pack loading is independent of hidden branch. Packs, source rights, measured bytes/durations, PCM/archive/delivery hashes and pending listening status are recorded separately.

Unit/authoring tests cover each mission's two routes in every world, incomplete/unsupported findings, matched twins, cutoffs, cache/idempotency, rejected authority, exact replay/import, response ties and future-only collection. Browser, offline, recovery, accessibility and production evidence is recorded in the verification ledger after execution. Code and manifest checks do not constitute human listening, art acceptance, physical-device evidence or a validated >9 player grade.
